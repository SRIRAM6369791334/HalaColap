## Welcome to the MSR Broilers Team!

This Quick Start Guide is designed to get you up and running with **Flockwise BizTrack** in less than 10 minutes. Follow these six essential steps to handle your daily duties efficiently and accurately.

---

## 1. How to Log In

1. Open **Google Chrome** (or your preferred web browser) on your computer.
2. Navigate to the internal application address provided by your manager (e.g., `http://localhost:8000`).
3. On the login screen:
   * Enter your registered **Email Address** or **Username**.
   * Enter your **Password**.
4. Click the green **Sign In** button.
5. If entered correctly, you will immediately arrive at the main **Dashboard**.

> [!TIP]
> If you type an incorrect password 10 times in a row, the system locks login attempts for 60 seconds to protect account security. If you forget your password, ask your farm administrator to reset it.

---

## 2. Understanding Your Dashboard

Your dashboard gives you a live look at farm performance:

* **Top Metric Cards:** Check **Today's Revenue**, **Active Batches** (live bird flocks), and **Pending Payments** (money owed by dealers).
* **Operational Alerts:** Look at the alerts section for upcoming loan EMIs, low feed stock warnings in godowns, or overdue dealer balances.
* **Recent Activity Feed:** See live records of recent bird dispatches and customer collections.

## 4. Your Daily Work Routine

Depending on your job role, follow this daily workflow:

### A. If You Are a Billing or Crate-Weighing Operator:
1. Open **Dayload Billing** from the sidebar.
2. Select today's **Billing Date**.
3. Click **Add Entry** (or **Bulk Entry**):
   * Select the supplying farm **Vendor** and destination **Dealer**.
   * Enter **No. of Boxes**, **Box Weight** (gross weight), and **Empty Weight** (empty crate tare weight).
   * Enter **Farm Weight** (weight leaving the farm) and the **Customer Rate** per kg.
   * The software automatically calculates net **Bird Weight**, **Loss Weight**, and **Total Amount**.
4. Once all trucks are loaded, ask the Manager to click **Approve Weight Loss**.
5. Click **Finalize Invoice** and print the delivery bills.

### B. If You Are a Warehouse or Farm Supervisor:
1. When feed or medicine trucks arrive, open **Operations → Purchases** to enter quantities and update warehouse stock.
2. When feed is distributed to chicken sheds, open **Inventory → Consumptions** and log the bags issued to that batch.
3. When inspecting sheds in the morning, count any dead birds and log them under **Inventory → Mortalities**.

### C. If You Are an Accounts or Cashier Staff:
1. When dealers pay via cash or UPI, open **Finance & Payments → Dealer Payments**.
2. Click **Record Payment**, select the dealer, and enter the amounts. The system automatically clears their oldest unpaid bills first (**FIFO**).
3. At the end of the day, open **Finance & Payments → Cash & Bank Ledger**, count physical cash in your drawer, verify it matches the screen, and have your manager **Approve Ledger**.

## 5. Common Actions across All Screens

* **Global Quick Search:** Press `Ctrl + K` (or `Cmd + K` on Mac) anywhere to quickly find any customer, dealer, invoice, or flock batch.
* **Add Record (+ Button):** Click the green button in the top-right corner of any screen to add a new entry.
* **Edit (Pencil Icon):** Update details for an existing record.
* **Delete (Trash Icon):** Click to remove a record. A confirmation pop-up will ask *"Are you sure?"* before deleting.
* **Print & Export:** Click **Print** for an immediate paper copy or **Export PDF** / **Export CSV** to download files for Excel.
* **Notifications Bell:** Check the bell icon in the top header for instant alerts on payments and stock levels.

---

## 6. How to Log Out Safely

Always log out when stepping away from your computer:
1. Move your mouse to the bottom of the left sidebar.
2. Look for the card showing your name and avatar.
3. Click the red **Logout** button (door icon).
4. The system ends your session and returns to the login screen.
